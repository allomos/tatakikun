# Symbol names (A02-town861)

Typing a symbol's name gives its symbols: the first in the candidate bar (4th place, after the best words), the rest at the top of the expanded list (↓). Source: `Sources/TatakikunCore/Symbols.swift`. Japanese names are kana readings, so any romaji spelling works (batu / batsu, sikaku / shikaku, ha-to); Spanish and French match with or without accents.
