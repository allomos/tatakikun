**Outcome.** Five more of the world's most spoken languages can be typed with Tatakikun, mixed into the candidates like English, Chinese and Japanese. Each one comes as a language pack that can be turned on or off.

**Done means.**
- For each new language: typing on a Latin keyboard gives correct candidates in that language, including whole words, and they mix with the other languages' candidates.
- Each language is its own pack, and the installer and settings can choose it.
- Tests cover common words in each language.

**Notes.**
- **Open for the person: which five.** Proposal, by total speakers (Ethnologue), after English, Chinese and Japanese: **Hindi, Spanish, Arabic, French, Bengali**. Next in line: Portuguese, Russian, Urdu, Indonesian.
- Spanish and French are typed in Latin letters: the work is word lists, plus accents (é, ñ) typed from plain letters. Hindi, Bengali (Devanagari/Bengali script) and Arabic (right-to-left) need typing by sound in Latin letters, like pinyin, so they are much larger. Arabic also needs right-to-left text checked in the candidate bar.
- Open for the person: the dictionary sources and their licences (they must allow MIT redistribution), and app size, since every pack makes the download bigger.
- Once the five are chosen, split this into one task per language.
