**Outcome.** On the Mac (a Japanese keyboard too), pressing a punctuation key no longer types one fixed mark: the mark shows as marked text with its alternatives as candidates (「 【 [ 『 ［ for [, 、 ， , for comma, 。 ． . for period), the usual one first; the next key confirms it.

**Done means.** Composer tests cover choosing a mark by digit, arrows, Space and by typing on; 3.14 and hello, still type straight through; the app is installed and a person types 「」 and 、。 in TextEdit.
